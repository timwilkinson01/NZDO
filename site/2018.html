2018 concert

The NZDO performed at Municipal Theatre, Napier on Sunday 24 June 2018, with José Aparicio as our conductor. There were 78 players.

Programme

  • Turina: Danzas Fantásticas
  • Schumann: Cello concerto, soloist Paul van Houtte (Auckland medical student)
  • Mussorgsky: Pictures at an Exhibition

$5,612.65 from ticket sales was donated to Cranford Hospice.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for dinner for the students.
