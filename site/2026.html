2026 concert

The NZDO performed at Theatre Royal, TSB Showplace, New Plymouth on Sunday 20 September 2026, with José Aparicio as our conductor. There were 82 players.

It was a sold-out performance.

Programme

  • Wagner: Tristan und Isolde – Prelude and Liebestod
  • Beethoven: Piano concerto 5 (Emperor), soloist Dr Ben Booker
  • Strauss: Tod und Verklärung

$9,091.60 from ticket sales was donated to Hospice Taranaki.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for some of our social functions.
