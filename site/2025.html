2025 concert

The NZDO performed at Nelson Centre of Musical Arts, Nelson on Sunday 20 July 2025, with Mark Hodgkinson as our conductor. There were 71 players.

It was a sold-out performance.

Programme

  • John Rimmer: Cloud Fanfares
  • Stravinsky: Firebird suite
  • Tchaikovsky: Violin concerto, soloist Dr Rachel Moxham

$6,883.25 from ticket sales was donated to Nelson Tasman Hospice.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for some of our social functions.
