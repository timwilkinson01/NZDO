2023 concert

The NZDO performed at Theatre Royal, TSB Showplace, New Plymouth on Sunday 23 July 2023, with Mark Hodgkinson as our conductor. There were 76 players.

Programme

  • Anthony Ritchie: Procession
  • Canteloube: Baïlèro from Songs of the Auvergne, soloist Dr Frances Campbell
  • Shostakovich: Symphony 5

$7,054.10 from ticket sales was donated to Hospice Taranaki.

We are grateful to MAS, who were our major sponsor. The University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and the University of Auckland provided sponsorship for student travel.
