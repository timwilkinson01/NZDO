2016 concert

The NZDO performed at Municipal Theatre, Napier on Sunday 26 June 2016, with José Aparicio as our conductor. There were 88 players.

Programme

  • Beethoven: Violin concerto, soloist Dr Kiarash Taghavi (paediatric surgical registrar)
  • Mahler: Symphony No 1

$8,383.80 from ticket sales was donated to Cranford Hospice.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for dinner for the students.
