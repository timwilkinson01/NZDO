A question worth asking

What helps people stay active after a stroke?

In 2014, Trust funding supported a Canterbury community stroke rehabilitation team to investigate how activity levels changed after hospital discharge. Research like this helps bring real-world questions into view—and gives health professionals evidence to consider in care.

Who we are

Local roots.
A shared purpose.

The Canterbury Health Care of the Elderly Education Trust is a Christchurch-based charitable trust. Its purpose is to strengthen research, education and the health workforce in older persons’ health care.

Trustee representation has included Age Concern, the University of Canterbury, the University of Otago, Ara Institute of Canterbury, Older Persons’ Health Service at Burwood Hospital and the community.
