2017 concert

The NZDO performed at The Piano, Christchurch on Sunday 11 June 2017, with Mark Hodgkinson as our conductor. There were 48 players.

Programme

  • Rossini: Overture to The Italian Girl in Algiers
  • Mozart: Concerto for flute and harp, soloists Dr Duncan Watts (Dunedin anaesthetist) and Dr Vanessa Souter (Wellington GP)
  • Beethoven: Symphony No 8
  • Dvořák: Czech Suite (final movement)

$6,894.90 from ticket sales was donated to Nurse Maude Hospice.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for dinner for the students.
