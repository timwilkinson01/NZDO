2014 concert

The NZDO performed at Theatre Royal, TSB Showplace, New Plymouth on Sunday 29 June 2014, with Mark Hodgkinson as our conductor. There were 71 players.

Programme

  • I never will – a commissioned work by one of our players, Dr Louise Webster
  • Mozart: Clarinet concerto (first movement), soloist Ryan Cha (final-year medical student)
  • Elgar: Pomp and Circumstance March No 1
  • Brahms: Symphony No 2

$4,122.60 from ticket sales was donated to Hospice Taranaki.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for dinner for the students.
