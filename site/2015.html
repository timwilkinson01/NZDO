2015 concert

The NZDO performed at Theatre Royal, TSB Showplace, New Plymouth on Sunday 28 June 2015, with Mark Hodgkinson as our conductor. There were 79 players.

Programme

  • Lilburn: Festival overture
  • Beethoven: Triple concerto, soloists Trio Pohádka (Dr Shyam Sankaran, Auckland radiology registrar; Lisa Chung & Petr Tomek)
  • Sibelius: Symphony No 2

$7,488.25 from ticket sales was donated to Hospice Taranaki.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for dinner for the students.
