2024 concert

The NZDO performed at Nelson Centre of Musical Arts, Nelson on Sunday 16 June 2024, with José Aparicio as our conductor. There were 67 players.

Programme

  • Tchaikovsky: Romeo and Juliet Overture
  • Brahms: Concerto for violin and cello, soloists Dr Osman Ozturk and Dr Catherine Kwak
  • Prokofiev: Romeo & Juliet Suite 2

$6,807.50 from ticket sales was donated to Nelson Tasman Hospice.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for some of our social functions.
