2022 concert

The NZDO performed at Nelson Centre of Musical Arts, Nelson on Sunday 24 July 2022, with Mark Hodgkinson as our conductor. There were 58 players.

Programme

  • Mozart: Piano concerto 24 in C minor K491, soloist Dr Louise Webster
  • Beethoven: Symphony 3

$3,495 from ticket sales was donated to Nelson Tasman Hospice.

We are grateful for sponsorship for student travel from the University of Otago division of humanities performing arts fund, and division of health sciences; and from the University of Auckland. MAS provided sponsorship for dinner for the students.
