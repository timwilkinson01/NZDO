What helps people stay active after a stroke?

In 2014, Trust funding supported a Canterbury community stroke rehabilitation team to investigate how activity levels changed after hospital discharge. Research like this helps bring real-world questions into view—and gives health professionals evidence to consider in care.

Who we are

Local roots.
A shared purpose.

The Canterbury Education and Research Trust for the Health of Older Persons is a Christchurch-based charitable trust. Under its 2021 deed, its objects include promoting teaching of older persons’ health across disciplines and in the community, supporting an academic department, promoting related research, and promoting and maintaining older people’s health and status.

The 2021 deed provides for representatives from the University of Otago Christchurch, New Zealand Association of Gerontology, Age Concern Canterbury, Canterbury District Health Board (or its successor), Ara Institute of Canterbury and the University of Canterbury, plus up to four other board members. Current trustees and successor organisations should be confirmed before publication.
