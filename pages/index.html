Once a year, doctors and medical students from across New Zealand come together to form a full symphony orchestra. We meet in a different part of the country each year for an intensive weekend of rehearsals, culminating in a public concert.

350+musicians have played with the NZDO

The NZDO provides an opportunity to make music at a high level, reconnect with colleagues and friends from around the country, meet new people and rediscover the pleasures of making music alongside a career in medicine.

Music that gives twice

Every NZDO player pays an enrolment fee that covers the costs of rehearsals, venue hire and the conductor. This means every dollar from ticket sales is donated to the local hospice in the city where we perform. By attending a concert, audiences support both outstanding live music and hospice care in their community.

Why do people play with the NZDO?

These are themes we hear repeatedly from NZDO players:

  • “The highlight of my year.”
  • “The NZDO got me playing again.”
  • “It showed me that medicine and music can coexist.”
  • “So much fun.”
  • “I’ve made great friends through the orchestra.”

Some players already play in other orchestras; others had stopped playing after school or university. The NZDO gives people a reason to pick up their instrument again and provides a way of combining music with a career in medicine.
